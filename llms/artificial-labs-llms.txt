# Artificial Labs

> Artificial Labs is a London-based insurance technology company building algorithmic and digital underwriting software for the specialty and reinsurance market, with the Lloyd's of London subscription market as its home ground. It sells Smart Underwriting (configurable lead-and-follow digital underwriting with appetite modelling and accept/refer/decline submission triage), Smart Placement (broker-side placement and distribution) and Contract Builder (MRCv3-compliant structured digital contract creation, which powers PPL's integrated Digital Contract Capability) to carriers, syndicates, MGAs and wholesale brokers.

Artificial Labs runs real production APIs and talks about them publicly, but does
not expose them publicly. There is no self-serve developer portal, no retrievable
OpenAPI or Swagger definition, no public API host, no public Postman collection,
no GraphQL endpoint, no MCP server and no event or webhook catalog. API
credentials are issued out of band under a partner or customer contract. The
documentation host docs.artificial.io answers HTTP 200 on its landing page only —
"To view the documentation, you must sign in" — and every product path 302s to an
Auth0 login at auth.artificialos.com. api.artificial.io and
developer.artificial.io have no DNS records. github.com/artificial-labs has zero
public repositories. This file records what is genuinely public.
